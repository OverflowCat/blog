TIL how to check symbols in OpenHarmony N-API binaries:

```sh
& "C:\Users\...\AppData\Local\OpenHarmony\Sdk\15\native\llvm\bin\llvm-strings.exe" "D:\...\entry\libs\arm64-v8a\libxxnapi.so" 2>&1 | Select-String "^some[regex]"
```
